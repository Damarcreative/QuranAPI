How to Install

  1. Download the appropriate APK file for your device architecture. (Most modern phones use arm64-v8a).
  2. If promoted, enable "Install from Unknown Sources" in your device settings.
  3. Open the downloaded file and tap "Install".
  4. Launch the app and enjoy ad-free Quran reading.
